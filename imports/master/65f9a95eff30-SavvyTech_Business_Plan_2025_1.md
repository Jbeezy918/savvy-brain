## Business Structure

### Recommended: LLC (Limited Liability Company)

**Why LLC?**
- ✅ Separates personal assets from business liability
- ✅ Tax flexibility (pass-through taxation)
- ✅ Professional credibility with clients
- ✅ Simple to set up and maintain
- ✅ Ideal for independent creators and small teams

**Action Items**:
1. ☐ Register LLC with your state (online filing, typically $50-$500)
2. ☐ Conduct trademark search for "SavvyTech Automations"
3. ☐ Apply for Federal Tax ID (EIN) via IRS.gov (free, takes 5 minutes)
4. ☐ Open dedicated business bank account (Chase, Mercury, or Relay recommended)
5. ☐ Get business insurance (general liability + E&O for consulting)
6. ☐ Register for any required state/local business licenses

## Risk Management

### Potential Risks & Mitigation

**1. Client Acquisition Cost Too High**
- Mitigation: Focus on referrals, content marketing, product-led growth
- Target: CAC < $500 (should recover in 1-2 months)

**2. Scope Creep on Fixed-Price Projects**
- Mitigation: Clear SOWs, time-boxed Jumpstart, move to retainers quickly
- Use contracts with defined deliverables

**3. Technology Changes (AI landscape evolving)**
- Mitigation: Stay current with AI developments, build modular systems
- Diversify: Don't rely on one AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.)

**4. Competition from Larger Agencies**
- Mitigation: Focus on niche, personal service, rapid delivery
- Emphasize "AI-first" and "built by AI agents"

**5. Cash Flow Issues**
- Mitigation: Require 50% deposits, monthly retainers upfront
- Maintain 3-6 months operating expenses in reserve
