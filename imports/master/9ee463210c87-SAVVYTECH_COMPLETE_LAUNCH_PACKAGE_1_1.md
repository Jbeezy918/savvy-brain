## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **1. Analysis Paralysis**
❌ Spending weeks perfecting the website/pitch/process
✅ Ship fast, iterate based on real feedback

### **2. Doing Everything Yourself**
❌ Trying to be designer, developer, marketer, salesperson
✅ Hire contractors for non-core tasks (design, bookkeeping)

### **3. Not Tracking Metrics**
❌ Flying blind, no idea what's working
✅ Update KPI dashboard daily, review weekly

### **4. Underpricing**
❌ Charging $50/hour because you're "just starting"
✅ Price based on value, not time (Jumpstart = $499, not $50)

### **5. No Sales Process**
❌ Winging discovery calls, forgetting to follow up
✅ Use scripts, templates, CRM (be systematic)

### **6. Ignoring Churn**
❌ Always chasing new clients, losing old ones
✅ Monthly check-ins, proactive support, upsells

### **7. Hiring Too Fast**
❌ Hiring full team in Month 1 (burn rate crisis)
✅ Hire contractors first, convert top performers

## 💡 Pro Tips from Successful AI Founders

### **Sales**
- **Offer free value first**: Free Jumpstart sessions → testimonials → paid clients
- **Focus on ROI**: "You'll save 20 hours/week" beats "We use cutting-edge AI"
- **Follow up relentlessly**: 80% of sales happen after 5+ touchpoints

### **Delivery**
- **Start with MVP**: Ship 80% solution in 2 weeks, iterate to 100%
- **Overcommunicate**: Weekly Loom updates > surprise delays
- **Document everything**: Future you (or your team) will thank you

### **Product**
- **Talk to users every week**: Don't build in a vacuum
- **Charge early**: Even $1/month validates demand
- **Iterate fast**: Weekly releases > quarterly big launches

### **Team**
- **Hire slow, fire fast**: Bad hires are expensive
- **Contractors first**: Test before committing to full-time
- **Culture = remote-first**: Global talent pool, lower costs
