# SavvyTech Automations - Launch Checklist
**90-Day Action Plan to Launch Your AI Business**

## 📋 Pre-Launch Foundation (Week 1-2)

### Legal & Business Structure

- [ ] **Choose business structure** (LLC recommended)
  - Review LLC benefits: liability protection, tax flexibility
  - Decide on single-member or multi-member LLC
  - Research your state's LLC filing requirements

- [ ] **Register your LLC**
  - File Articles of Organization with your state
  - Cost: $50-$500 depending on state
  - Options: DIY (state website), LegalZoom, Northwest Registered Agent
  - Timeline: 1-2 weeks for approval

- [ ] **Create Operating Agreement**
  - Define ownership structure
  - Outline profit distribution
  - Document decision-making process
  - Template available from LegalZoom or local attorney

- [ ] **Conduct trademark search**
  - Search USPTO database: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks
  - Check for "SavvyTech Automations" conflicts
  - Reserve name if clear
  - Consider filing trademark ($250-$750)

- [ ] **Apply for EIN (Employer Identification Number)**
  - Free application via IRS.gov
  - Takes 5 minutes online
  - Required for bank account and taxes
  - Link: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online

---

### Financial Setup

- [ ] **Open business bank account**
  - Recommended options:
    - **Mercury**: Tech-friendly, online, no fees
    - **Chase Business**: Traditional, in-person support
    - **Relay**: Multiple accounts, free, great for tracking
  - Required documents: EIN, LLC paperwork, ID

- [ ] **Set up accounting system**
  - **Option 1: Wave** (free, simple, good for startups)
  - **Option 2: QuickBooks** ($30/mo, robust features)
  - **Option 3: Xero** ($15/mo, modern interface)
  - Connect to bank account for auto-sync
  - Set up expense categories

- [ ] **Get business credit card**
  - Separate business expenses from personal
  - Build business credit
  - Recommended: Chase Ink, American Express Business
  - Track all automation tool subscriptions here

- [ ] **Set up invoicing system**
  - Integrated with accounting (QuickBooks, Wave)
  - Or standalone: Bonsai, FreshBooks, Invoice Ninja
  - Create invoice template with logo and branding
  - Set up payment processing (Stripe, PayPal, Square)

---

### Insurance & Risk Management

- [ ] **Get business insurance**
  - **General Liability**: $1M-$2M coverage ($30-$100/mo)
  - **Professional Liability (E&O)**: $1M coverage ($50-$150/mo)
  - **Cyber Liability**: Optional but recommended for data work
  - Recommended providers: Hiscox, Next Insurance, Embroker
  - Quote online in 10 minutes

- [ ] **Create client service agreement template**
  - Define scope, deliverables, timeline
  - Payment terms (50% deposit, 50% on completion)
  - Liability limitations, indemnification
  - IP ownership and licensing
  - Template source: Bonsai, Rocket Lawyer, or local attorney

- [ ] **Draft NDA template**
  - For sensitive client projects
  - Mutual or one-way depending on situation
  - Template available from Rocket Lawyer

## 📞 Sales Process (Week 1 onwards)

### Lead Generation

- [ ] **Build prospect list**
  - LinkedIn Sales Navigator (free trial or $79/mo)
  - Target: Small business owners, ops managers, CTOs
  - Goal: 100+ qualified leads

- [ ] **Outreach sequence (Cold Email)**
  - Email 1: Intro + value prop (Day 1)
  - Email 2: Case study + social proof (Day 4)
  - Email 3: Specific offer (Jumpstart) (Day 7)
  - Email 4: Last attempt (Day 14)
  - Tool: Mailshake, Lemlist, or manual

- [ ] **Outreach sequence (LinkedIn)**
  - Connection request with note
  - Message 1: Intro (Day 1)
  - Message 2: Share case study (Day 3)
  - Message 3: Offer free consult (Day 7)

---

### Sales Calls

- [ ] **Create discovery call script**
  - Intro (2 min): Who you are, what SavvyTech does
  - Questions (10 min): Pain points, current process, goals
  - Demo (5 min): Show relevant example or tool
  - Pitch (5 min): Recommend Jumpstart or service package
  - Close (3 min): Next steps, send proposal
  - Total: 25-30 minutes

- [ ] **Book first 10 discovery calls**
  - Offer: Free 15-minute automation audit
  - Use Calendly link in all outreach
  - Goal: 10% conversion to Jumpstart

---

### Closing

- [ ] **Send proposals within 24 hours**
  - Use template, customize for each client
  - Include ROI projections
  - Add urgency: "Book by [date] for 10% discount"

- [ ] **Follow-up sequence**
  - Day 1: Send proposal
  - Day 3: Check-in email
  - Day 7: Call or video message
  - Day 14: Last attempt with discount or deadline
