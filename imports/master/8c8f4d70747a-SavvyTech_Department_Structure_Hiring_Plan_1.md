# SavvyTech Automations - Department Structure & Hiring Plan
**Scaling from Solo to Team: 0 → 10 Employees in Year 1-2**

---

## 🎯 Overview

This document outlines the department structure, hiring sequence, role definitions, and operational playbooks for scaling SavvyTech Automations from a solo founder to a fully operational team.

**Hiring Philosophy**:
- Hire slow, fire fast
- Start with contractors/fractional roles
- Convert top performers to full-time
- Remote-first, global talent pool
- Culture fit > resume

---

## 🚀 Hiring Sequence & Timeline

### **Phase 1: Solo Founder (Months 1-3)**

**You do everything**:
- ✅ Sales (Jumpstart calls, proposals)
- ✅ Delivery (build automations, apps)
- ✅ Marketing (website, content, ads)
- ✅ Finance (invoicing, bookkeeping)
- ✅ Support (client communication)

**Goal**: Prove the model, get first 5-10 clients, $10K+ MRR

**Tools**:
- CRM: HubSpot (free)
- Project Management: Notion
- Accounting: QuickBooks or Wave
- Communication: Slack, Calendly, Loom

---

### **Phase 2: First Hires (Months 4-6) - Revenue: $10K-$20K/month**

#### **Hire #1: Fractional Bookkeeper/Accountant** (Month 4)
**Type**: Part-time contractor (5-10 hours/month)
**Cost**: $500-$1,000/month
**Why First**: Frees you from financial admin, ensures compliance

**Responsibilities**:
- Monthly bookkeeping (reconcile accounts, categorize expenses)
- Quarterly tax prep and filing
- Financial reporting (P&L, cash flow)
- Payroll setup (when you hire employees)

**Where to Find**:
- Upwork, Bench, Pilot
- Local CPA firms (fractional services)

**KPIs**:
- Books closed by 10th of each month
- Zero late tax filings
- Monthly financial dashboard delivered

---

#### **Hire #2: BDR/SDR (Sales)** (Month 5)
**Type**: Full-time contractor → Convert to employee if hits targets
**Cost**: $3,000-$5,000/month base + 10% commission
**Why**: You're spending 50%+ of time on sales; need to focus on delivery

**Responsibilities**:
- Prospect 100+ leads/week (LinkedIn, cold email)
- Qualify leads and book Jumpstart calls
- Run initial discovery calls (hand off to you for close)
- Follow up with proposals and contracts
- Build partner/referral network

**Ideal Profile**:
- 1-2 years B2B sales experience
- Comfortable with automation/SaaS
- Self-starter, coachable
- Strong written and verbal communication

**Where to Find**:
- LinkedIn (search "SDR" + "SaaS" + "automation")
- AngelList, We Work Remotely
- Referrals from other founders

**KPIs**:
- 20+ qualified leads/month
- 10+ Jumpstart calls booked/month
- 30%+ conversion to paid Jumpstart
- 5+ paying clients/month by Month 3

**Compensation Structure**:
- Base: $3,000/month
- Commission: 10% of first payment (Jumpstart, recurring setup fees)
- Bonus: $500 for every 5 Jumpstart clients closed in a month

---

#### **Hire #3: AI Engineer/Full-Stack Dev** (Month 6)
**Type**: Full-time contractor → Employee
**Cost**: $5,000-$8,000/month (or $60-$100/hour)
**Why**: You can't do all client delivery + product development

**Responsibilities**:
- Build client automations (chatbots, integrations, dashboards)
- Develop Spark Tracker, AI Relay, Bolt
- Maintain existing client projects
- Technical documentation
- Code reviews and QA

**Ideal Profile**:
- 3+ years full-stack development (Python, JavaScript/TypeScript)
- Experience with AI APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.)
- Automation tools (Zapier, Make, n8n)
- Can work independently, communicate progress

**Where to Find**:
- Upwork, Toptal, Gun.io (vetted freelancers)
- LinkedIn, AngelList
- Indie Hackers, HackerNews "Who's Hiring?"

**KPIs**:
- 80% of client projects delivered on time
- <5% bug rate post-launch
- 2+ product features shipped/month
- Client satisfaction: 4.5+/5

**Compensation Structure**:
- Contractor: $75/hour, 40 hours/week = $12K/month
- Employee: $90K-$120K/year salary + equity (0.5-1%)

---

### **Phase 3: Scale Team (Months 7-12) - Revenue: $20K-$50K/month**

#### **Hire #4: Client Success/Onboarding Specialist** (Month 7)
**Type**: Part-time contractor → Full-time
**Cost**: $2,500-$4,000/month (part-time) or $50K-$65K/year (full-time)
**Why**: Churn prevention, free up time for high-value work

**Responsibilities**:
- Onboard new Jumpstart/Essentials/Growth clients
- Create training videos and documentation
- Weekly check-ins with active clients
- Handle support tickets (email, Slack)
- Upsell Growth → Pro Managed
- Track client satisfaction (NPS)

**Ideal Profile**:
- Customer success or account management experience
- Tech-savvy, can explain automations to non-technical users
- Patient, detail-oriented
- Strong writing skills (for docs and emails)

**Where to Find**:
- LinkedIn, Indeed
- Customer Success communities (Gain Grow Retain)
- Remote job boards

**KPIs**:
- <5% monthly churn
- 90%+ client onboarding completion rate
- NPS: 50+
- 20%+ upsell conversion (Growth → Pro)

---

#### **Hire #5: Marketing Lead** (Month 9)

**Cost**: $4,000-$6,000/month or $60K-$80K/year
**Why**: Need consistent content, campaigns, and lead generation

**Ideal Profile**:
- 2-3 years B2B marketing experience (SaaS/automation)
- Strong writer, comfortable on camera
- Data-driven (knows Google Analytics, ad platforms)

**Where to Find**:
- LinkedIn, AngelList
- Marketing communities (GrowthHackers, Indie Hackers)
- Referrals from other SaaS founders

**KPIs**:
- 1,000+ website visitors/month by Month 3
- 50+ qualified leads/month from organic + paid
- 10+ case studies/testimonials published
- CAC < $500

**Compensation Structure**:

- Employee: $70K/year + 10% bonus tied to lead generation

## 📋 Hiring Process (Standard for All Roles)

### Step 1: Job Post (1 week)
- Write compelling job description
- Post to: LinkedIn, AngelList, We Work Remotely, Upwork
- Include: Role, responsibilities, ideal profile, compensation range
- Deadline: 1 week to apply

### Step 2: Resume Review (3 days)
- Screen 20-50 applicants
- Look for: Relevant experience, culture fit, writing quality
- Shortlist: Top 5-10 candidates

### Step 3: Initial Screen (30 min, video call)
- Ask: Background, why interested, availability, salary expectations
- Assess: Communication, enthusiasm, culture fit
- Shortlist: Top 3 candidates

### Step 4: Skills Assessment (Take-home or live)
- **Sales**: Mock discovery call (record on Loom)
- **Engineering**: Code challenge or past work review
- **Marketing**: Write sample blog post or ad campaign
- **Client Success**: Handle mock support ticket

### Step 5: Final Interview (60 min)
- Deep dive on experience
- Scenario-based questions (how would you handle X?)
- Meet the team (if applicable)
- Q&A

### Step 6: Reference Checks
- Call 2 previous managers or colleagues
- Ask: Strengths, weaknesses, work ethic, culture fit

### Step 7: Offer
- Send written offer (Bonsai, Deel, or email)
- Include: Role, compensation, start date, equity (if applicable)
- Deadline: 3-5 days to accept

### Step 8: Onboarding (First 30 days)
- Day 1: Welcome, tools access, team intro
- Week 1: Training, shadow calls/projects
- Week 2: First solo project (with support)
- Week 4: 30-day check-in, feedback, adjust

## 🎯 Department-Specific Playbooks

### **1. Sales & Partnerships**

**Daily Tasks (BDR/SDR)**:
- 9:00 AM: Review leads in CRM, prioritize outreach
- 9:30 AM: Send 20 personalized LinkedIn messages
- 10:30 AM: Send 30 cold emails (sequences)
- 12:00 PM: Follow up on active conversations
- 1:00 PM: Run 2-3 discovery calls
- 3:00 PM: Send proposals, update CRM
- 4:00 PM: Prospecting research (build new lists)

**Weekly Tasks**:
- Monday: Set weekly targets (leads, calls, closes)
- Wednesday: Mid-week review with manager
- Friday: Weekly report, pipeline review

**Monthly KPIs**:
- 100+ new leads added to CRM
- 40+ discovery calls completed
- 20+ proposals sent
- 10+ Jumpstart clients closed
- $5K+ in new MRR

**Tools**:
- CRM: HubSpot, Pipedrive
- Outreach: LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Lemlist, Mailshake
- Calendar: Calendly
- Communication: Slack, Loom

### **2. AI Product Development**

**Daily Standup** (Async in Slack):
- What I did yesterday
- What I'm doing today
- Any blockers

**Sprint Cycle** (2 weeks):
- Week 1: Build features, fix bugs
- Week 2: Testing, code review, deploy
- Friday: Sprint retro, plan next sprint

**Client Projects**:
- Discovery: Understand requirements, scope
- Design: Architecture, mockups, approval
- Build: Development in 1-week sprints
- Test: QA, client review, feedback
- Launch: Deploy, training, handoff to Client Success

**Product Development**:
- Roadmap: Quarterly planning (Notion/Linear)
- Sprints: 2-week cycles
- Releases: Monthly product updates

**Monthly KPIs**:
- 90%+ projects delivered on time
- <3% bug rate post-launch
- 4+ product features shipped
- Client satisfaction: 4.5+/5

**Tools**:
- Project Management: Linear, Jira, Notion

- CI/CD: Vercel, Netlify, Railway
- Communication: Slack, Loom

- Day 1: Welcome email, Slack invite, calendar intro call

- Day 30: NPS survey, upsell to Growth/Pro

**Support Process**:
- Tier 1 (Simple): Client Success answers (< 4 hours)
- Tier 2 (Technical): Escalate to Engineering
- Tier 3 (Critical): All hands on deck

**Weekly Check-Ins**:
- Monday: Review active clients, flag at-risk accounts
- Wednesday: Reach out to quiet clients (proactive)
- Friday: Update churn dashboard, plan outreach

**Monthly KPIs**:
- <5% churn rate
- 90%+ onboarding completion
- NPS: 50+
- 20%+ upsell rate (Growth → Pro)

**Tools**:
- Support: Intercom, Zendesk, Help Scout
- Video: Loom, Vidyard
- Docs: Notion, GitBook
- Communication: Slack, email

## 📊 Daily Standup & KPI Tracking

### **Daily Standup** (15 min, 9:00 AM)

**Format** (Async in Slack or live on Zoom):
- **What I did yesterday**
- **What I'm doing today**
- **Any blockers**

**Who Attends**:
- Founder
- All full-time team members

**Rules**:
- Keep it short (1-2 sentences per person)
- Focus on progress, not excuses
- Blockers get resolved after standup (1-on-1)
