## 📞 Sales Playbook (Discovery Call Script)

### **Pre-Call Preparation** (5 min)
- [ ] Review prospect's LinkedIn profile
- [ ] Check company website (understand business)
- [ ] Prepare 3 custom questions based on their industry

---

### **Discovery Call** (25-30 min)

#### **Intro (2 min)**
> "Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time. I'm [Your Name], founder of SavvyTech Automations. We help businesses like yours automate repetitive processes so you can focus on growth. Before I share how we work, I'd love to learn about you and your business. Sound good?"

#### **Discovery Questions (10 min)**
Ask open-ended questions, listen 80% of the time:

1. **"Tell me about your business. What do you do?"**
   - Listen for: Industry, size, revenue, team structure

2. **"What made you interested in automation?"**
   - Listen for: Pain points, specific processes they want to automate

3. **"Walk me through a typical day. What tasks eat up most of your time?"**
   - Listen for: Repetitive tasks, manual data entry, copy-paste workflows

4. **"If you could wave a magic wand and automate one thing tomorrow, what would it be?"**
   - Listen for: Highest priority, biggest pain point

5. **"What have you tried so far? Any automation tools or consultants?"**
   - Listen for: Past attempts, budget, decision-making process

6. **"What does success look like for you? How would you measure ROI?"**
   - Listen for: Goals (time saved, revenue increase, error reduction)

#### **Demo / Solution Preview (5 min)**
Based on their answers, show 1 relevant example:

> "Great, based on what you shared, here's how we'd approach it. [Screen share or describe] We recently built something similar for [Client]. They were spending 20 hours/week on [X], now it's fully automated. It took us 3 weeks to build and they saw ROI in 6 weeks."

#### **Pitch Jumpstart (5 min)**
> "Here's how we work: We start with our Jumpstart package—$499 one-time. It's a 90-minute workshop where we map your processes, identify the best automation opportunities, and give you a detailed ROI plan. We'll also build a quick-win prototype so you see value immediately. Then, if you want us to build the full solution, we'll provide a custom quote. No obligation. Does that sound like a good next step?"

#### **Objection Handling**

**"That's expensive."**
> "I totally get it. Let me ask—how much time are you spending on [X] each week? [Calculate] At your hourly rate, that's [$XXX] per month. The Jumpstart pays for itself in the first month, and the automation saves you [$XXX] every month after. Does that make sense?"

**"I need to think about it."**
> "Of course. What specifically would you like to think about? Budget, timeline, or the process itself? I'm happy to answer any questions now."

**"I want to talk to my team first."**
> "Smart move. How about this—I'll send you a quick one-pager summarizing what we discussed, plus a case study. You share it with your team, and we'll schedule a follow-up call next week. Sound good?"

#### **Close (3 min)**
> "Great! Let's get you on the calendar. I'll send over a link to book your Jumpstart session. You'll get a calendar invite and a pre-call questionnaire so we can hit the ground running. Sound good?"

**Send immediately after call**:
- [ ] Calendar link (Calendly)
- [ ] One-pager (Jumpstart overview)
- [ ] Case study or testimonial
- [ ] Follow-up email (confirm next steps)

## 🔨 Client Delivery Playbook

### **Phase 1: Discovery (Week 1)**

#### Kickoff Call (60 min)
**Agenda**:
1. Introductions (5 min)
2. Client goals and challenges (20 min)
3. Current process walkthrough (20 min)
4. Desired future state (10 min)
5. Next steps and timeline (5 min)

**Deliverables**:
- [ ] Process map (current state)
- [ ] Requirements doc (what needs to be built)
- [ ] Timeline and milestones

---

### **Phase 2: Design (Week 2)**

#### Design Review Call (30 min)
**Agenda**:
1. Present architecture/wireframes (15 min)
2. Walk through user flow (10 min)
3. Q&A and adjustments (5 min)

**Deliverables**:
- [ ] Architecture diagram (tools, APIs, workflows)
- [ ] Mockups or wireframes (Figma, Whimsical)
- [ ] ROI projection (time saved, cost saved)

**Approval Required**: Client signs off on design before build

---

### **Phase 3: Build (Weeks 3-4)**

#### Weekly Check-ins (15 min)
- [ ] Share progress update (Loom video or screen share)
- [ ] Demo what's working so far
- [ ] Address questions or change requests

**Deliverables**:
- [ ] Working prototype (by end of Week 3)
- [ ] Final build (by end of Week 4)

---

### **Phase 4: Testing & Launch (Week 5)**

#### Testing Session (60 min)
- [ ] Client tests automation/app
- [ ] Note bugs, feedback, adjustments
- [ ] Fix and retest

#### Launch Day
- [ ] Deploy to production
- [ ] Train client (60 min Zoom call or pre-recorded Loom)
- [ ] Provide documentation (written guide + video)

**Deliverables**:
- [ ] Live automation/app
- [ ] Training video (Loom)
- [ ] Documentation (Notion, Google Doc)

---

### **Phase 5: Support (Ongoing)**

#### 30-Day Check-in
- [ ] How's it going? Any issues?
- [ ] Usage stats (if applicable)
- [ ] Optimization opportunities
- [ ] Upsell to recurring plan (if Jumpstart client)

#### Ongoing Support (Growth/Pro clients)
- [ ] Monthly check-ins (30 min)
- [ ] Respond to support tickets (<4 hours)
- [ ] Feature enhancements (as needed)

## 🔥 When You Feel Stuck

### **Low Motivation?**
- [ ] Review why you started (vision, goals)
- [ ] Celebrate a recent win (no matter how small)
- [ ] Take a 20-minute walk
- [ ] Work on the easiest task on your list

### **Too Many Tasks?**
- [ ] Ruthlessly prioritize (What moves revenue needle?)
- [ ] Delegate or delete non-essentials
- [ ] Time-block your calendar
- [ ] Say "no" to distractions

### **No Sales This Week?**
- [ ] Double outreach efforts (40 messages, 60 emails)
- [ ] Review messaging (is it clear? compelling?)
- [ ] Offer limited-time discount (e.g., "$100 off if you book this week")
- [ ] Ask current clients for referrals

### **Client Projects Behind Schedule?**
- [ ] Communicate early (don't wait until deadline)
- [ ] Renegotiate timeline if needed
- [ ] Focus on MVP (cut scope, ship faster)
- [ ] Consider hiring contractor to help
