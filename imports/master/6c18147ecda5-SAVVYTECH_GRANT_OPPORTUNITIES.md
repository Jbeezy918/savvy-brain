## CERTIFICATIONS TO GET (Opens More Doors)

### HUBZone Certification
- **Why:** Federal contracting preference, some grants require it
- **Cost:** Free
- **Apply:** https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/hubzone-program
- **You Qualify:** ✅ YES - McAlester is HUBZone eligible

### 8(a) Business Development Program
- **Why:** Federal contracting set-asides, mentorship
- **Cost:** Free
- **Apply:** https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/8a-business-development-program
- **You Qualify:** ⚠️ MAYBE - Minority-owned, must prove social/economic disadvantage
- **Note:** Program has had recent policy changes in 2026

### SAM.gov Registration
- **Why:** Required for ALL federal grants and contracts
- **Cost:** Free
- **Apply:** https://sam.gov/
- **Timeline:** Takes 4-6 weeks - START NOW if not registered
